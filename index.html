Ranking customization lab

Build Your Draft Board

A bundled Sleeper ADP snapshot is the default market baseline. Upload rankings and projections from any sites you trust. The Lab normalizes available projections into league-specific value over replacement, separates conviction from market cost, and estimates how your actual room changes the acquisition window. Everything runs in this browser—no Node.js, database, API keys, or launcher required.

How to get better Lab Analysis

Upload at least two independent ranking sources to measure agreement. Add projection and projection_period (weekly or season) to retain source projections; projection-only rows do not become ranking sources. Optional context columns such as summary, depth_chart_role, upside_note, risk_note, and tags create player-specific reasons.

Example tags: three_down|receiving_back|goal_line, target_hog|deep_threat|red_zone, or route_heavy|slot|target_competition.
